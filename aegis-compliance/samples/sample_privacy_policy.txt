Acme AI, Inc. — Privacy Policy (sample text for offline testing)

We collect account information, usage logs, and the content you submit to our
AI assistant. We use this information to operate the service, improve model
quality, and communicate with you.

Automated tools help us screen support tickets and rank candidates in our
recruiting pipeline. We do not currently offer a way to opt out of these
automated processes, and we do not describe how a person can request human
review of an automated decision.

Our models are 100% unbiased and fully HIPAA compliant. Customer prompts may be
processed by third-party LLM providers for inference and model training.

We conduct annual security reviews and follow industry best practices for
protecting customer data.
